MICROSOFT HR POLICY ASSISTANT
PUBLICLY AVAILABLE MICROSOFT HR / EMPLOYEE INFORMATION

IMPORTANT:
This dataset contains information publicly available from official Microsoft
websites. It is NOT Microsoft's complete internal HR handbook. Some detailed
employee policies are internal or location-specific. The assistant must not
invent missing policies.

============================================================
1. COMPANY OVERVIEW
============================================================

Company:
Microsoft Corporation

Microsoft's mission is to empower every person and every organization on
the planet to achieve more.

Microsoft describes its culture around a growth mindset, customer obsession,
One Microsoft, diversity and inclusion, and making a difference.

Microsoft's Standards of Business Conduct / Trust Code emphasizes:
- Respect
- Integrity
- Accountability

Microsoft Standards of Business Conduct
https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/legal/compliance/sbc/standards

3. TIME OFF AND LEAVE
============================================================

Microsoft publicly describes several time-away programs.

These include:
- Vacation
- Sick time
- Holidays
- Parental leave
- Family caregiver leave
- Military leave
- Bereavement leave
- Jury duty
- Unpaid personal leave

Exact eligibility and entitlement depend on employee classification,
location and applicable benefits plans.

Microsoft does NOT have one publicly stated universal leave entitlement
for every employee worldwide.

SOURCE:
Microsoft U.S. Benefits
https://usbenefits.microsoft.com/us/en/leave-of-absence.html


============================================================
4. U.S. PARENTAL LEAVE
============================================================

For eligible U.S. Microsoft employees, Microsoft states that new parents
can take up to 12 weeks off.

Birth mothers/persons may be eligible for an additional eight weeks of
maternity disability leave.

This can result in up to 20 weeks for eligible birth mothers/persons.

These figures are U.S.-specific and subject to eligibility requirements.

SOURCE:
Microsoft U.S. Benefits - Leave of Absence
https://usbenefits.microsoft.com/us/en/leave-of-absence.html


============================================================
5. U.S. FAMILY CAREGIVER LEAVE
============================================================

For eligible U.S. employees, Microsoft states that family caregiver leave
can provide up to 12 weeks.

Microsoft states:
- First 4 weeks: paid at 100% of salary
- Remaining 8 weeks: unpaid

Eligibility and applicable plan requirements apply.

These provisions should NOT automatically be applied to employees outside
the United States.

SOURCE:
Microsoft U.S. Benefits - Leave of Absence
https://usbenefits.microsoft.com/us/en/leave-of-absence.html


============================================================
6. U.S. SHORT-TERM DISABILITY
============================================================

Microsoft's public U.S. benefits information describes short-term
disability leave of up to 26 weeks.

The public information states:
- First 8 weeks: 100% of salary
- Remaining weeks: 75% of salary

Eligibility and applicable policy requirements apply.

This information is U.S.-specific.

SOURCE:
Microsoft U.S. Benefits - Leave of Absence
https://usbenefits.microsoft.com/us/en/leave-of-absence.html


============================================================
7. U.S. MILITARY LEAVE
============================================================

For eligible U.S. employees serving in the military, Microsoft states
that employees can receive up to 30 days of full pay without offset per
calendar year for military duty.

Additional provisions may apply.

This information is U.S.-specific.

SOURCE:
Microsoft U.S. Benefits - Leave of Absence
https://usbenefits.microsoft.com/us/en/leave-of-absence.html


============================================================
8. VACATION / DISCRETIONARY TIME OFF
============================================================

Microsoft's U.S. benefits information describes vacation and
Discretionary Time Off programs.

The applicable program depends on employee eligibility and classification.

Microsoft also publicly describes floating holidays for eligible
employees.

For example, the U.S. benefits information states that eligible
employees may receive four floating holidays per year.

Part-time employee benefits may be prorated according to applicable
rules.

This information is U.S.-specific.

SOURCE:
Microsoft U.S. Benefits - Vacation / DTO
https://usbenefits.microsoft.com/us/en/vacation-dto.html

16. CONFLICTS OF INTEREST
============================================================

Microsoft's Trust Code provides guidance regarding conflicts of interest.

Employees should act in Microsoft's best interests and avoid situations
where personal relationships or financial interests could improperly
influence business decisions.

Potential conflicts should be disclosed and employees should seek
appropriate advice or approval.

Outside activities should not interfere with Microsoft's interests or
violate applicable agreements and policies.

SOURCE:
Microsoft Standards of Business Conduct
https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/legal/compliance/sbc/standards

20. COMPLIANCE AND ETHICS
============================================================

Microsoft describes its compliance and ethics program around three
major areas:

1. Prevention
2. Detection
3. Remediation

Prevention activities can include:
- Standards of Business Conduct
- Policies
- Training
- Risk assessment
- Data analytics
- Third-party vetting

Detection activities can include:
- Internal audit testing
- Compliance analytics
- Controls
- Investigations
- Trend analysis

Remediation focuses on identifying root causes and improving controls
and processes.

Microsoft states that employees who violate its policies and standards
can face disciplinary action regardless of their level.

Disciplinary action can extend up to and including termination of
employment.

SOURCE:
Microsoft Legal - Compliance
https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/legal/compliance

26. IMPORTANT REGION RULE
============================================================

U.S.-specific benefits must not automatically be applied to employees
in another country.

For example:

Question:
"How much parental leave does a Microsoft employee in India receive?"

The assistant should NOT answer using the U.S. 12-week rule.

Correct response:
"The available public Microsoft information in this knowledge base
contains U.S.-specific parental leave information. It does not establish
a universal entitlement for Microsoft employees in India."
